Dogs have a sense of time. They notice and predict daily routines like walks and feeding times.

A dog's nose print is completely unique, similar to a human fingerprint, and can be used for identification.
The Basenji is the only breed of dog that does not bark; instead, they make a yodeling sound.
Dogs have three eyelids, including a special membrane called a nictitating membrane that helps protect and moisten the eye.
Greyhounds are the fastest dogs in the world, capable of reaching speeds up to 45 miles per hour.
Dogs sweat only through their paws. They rely on panting to cool themselves down.
Puppies are born blind, deaf, and toothless, relying entirely on their mother's warmth and sense of smell.
The oldest known dog on record was an Australian Cattle Dog named Bluey, who lived to be 29 years and 5 months old.
All domestic dogs are descendants of wild wolves, sharing 99% of their DNA.
Dogs have about 1,700 taste buds, compared to a human's 9,000 taste buds.
A dog's whiskers are highly sensitive touch-receptors called vibrissae, helping them navigate in the dark.
Dogs can hear sounds at frequencies up to 45,000 Hz, whereas humans can only hear up to 20,000 Hz.
